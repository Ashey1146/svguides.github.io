Supervision & Preparation Guidelines

Mandatory setup requirements before starting any session

Supervision Requirements

Hosts and Co-Hosts who hold ranks of Superintendent or Chief of Staff require active supervision from a higher-ranking staff member unless a Board of Directors+ member is present.

Schedule & Setup Timeline

Co-Hosts must configure session records on Bloxcord prior to opening. The Host unlocks the session room at XX:45 and locks the doors at XX:00 sharp.

Readiness Check

Verify that your Co-Host, Trainers, and Overseers are fully set up in position before announcing rule breakdowns or commencing session phases.

Locking Announcement Line

H The session room has been locked. Remaining trainees may now enter standard training procedures.

Section Divider: STARTING

Session Introduction & Rule Breakdown

Phase 1: Startup Script
Host Welcome Speech

"Hello, welcome to today's training! My name is [Host Username], and I will be your host."

Co-Host Welcome Speech

"Hey! I'm [Co-Host Username], your co-host, and I'll be assisting in today's training."

Standard Session Rules (Host / Co-Host Transcript)

Read in sequential order
Rule 1: You must refer to me and my Co-Host by our ranks at all times.
Rule 2: Do not go AFK (Away From Keyboard) or ask PTS (Permission To Speak) while I or my Co-Host are speaking. Doing so will result in a strike or removal.
Rule 3: You must be respectful to everyone at all times. Failure to do so will result in training removal and further sanctions.
Rule 4: If there is any problem, come to me, my Co-Host, or any other Sir/Ma'am for help. Do not ask for promotion/rank up, or you will be instantly removed.
Rule 5: Trolling in any form is unacceptable. Grammar must be used throughout this training. Failure or failure to use grammar will result in a strike or removal from the training.
Rule 6: PTS is active unless stated otherwise by me or my Co-Host. Is that understood?
Section Divider: FACES

Drill & Facing Commands Evaluation

Phase 2: Reaction time & directional listening orientation

Phase 2
Phase Introduction Speech

"We will begin with the faces. In this phase, we will be testing your reaction time and listening skills. Please listen carefully as I will only explain each face once. DO NOT actually do the faces at this point."

Left Face

Turn left 90 degrees from your current position.

Right Face

Turn right 90 degrees from your current position.

Center Face

Turn to your normal position (the position you are in right now).

About Face

Turn 180 degrees from your current position.

Control Face

Face the Host/Co-Host at all times (they should be centered on screen at all times). Note: Only another face breaks Control Face; Center Face does not.

Session Tip: Trainees who fail reaction speed or direction twice are dismissed. Remaining attendees standing at completion are marked as passers on Bloxcord.
Section Divider: QUESTIONS

Rules & Knowledge Assessment Phase

Phase 3: PM Testing for grammar & institutional protocol

Phase 3

Instructional Speech Sequence

"We are now moving on to questions. In this phase, I'll be testing you on your knowledge of the rules, grammar, and prison guidelines."

"To start this off, I will send you a private message with the questions I will state shortly. DO NOT send your answers yet."

"I will state the questions in Channel 2:00. DO NOT say the answers out loud—only reply to my PM."

Question 1

"What is one reason to use the medical system?"

Question 2

"What would you do if you see a user breaking Roblox Terms of Service?"

Timer Command: "You have 2 minutes, starting now!"
Section Divider: SCENARIOS

Practical Tactical Evaluation

Phase 4: Equipment usage, comprehension, & threat response

Phase 4

3 Essential Rules for Practical Scenarios

Mandatory Safety Guidelines
1. No Lethal Force

DO NOT actually fragg or kill the inmates. Verbal commands and non-lethal equipment apply.

2. Boundary Control

DO NOT step outside the designated yellow boundary box at any time during execution.

3. Authorized Tools Only

Tools permitted: Handcuffs, Taser, Glock, Shield. Unapproved gear results in instant failure.

Simulated Event Expected Trainee Action Standard Outcome
Inmate shoots an officer Neutralize threat, cuff inmate, transfer to maximum security PASS
Inmate exploiting / wall-clipping Record 30s clip, report to game report ticket server PASS
Disrespectful community guidelines violation Warn twice, record video evidence, submit report ticket PASS
Section Divider: ACCURACY

Firearms Accuracy & Target Assessment

Phase 5: Shooting range precision test

Phase 5
Range Instruction Speech

"You are only allowed to use the Glock 17 for this phase, and you must stay behind the red line at all times. Failure to follow these rules will result in failing this phase. Without further ado, please relocate to the blue instructional line behind the host's line."

Session Efficiency Tip: Call up the next participant while the current trainee is taking their accuracy test to keep the session moving rapidly.
Section Divider: ENDING

Session Conclusion & Dismissal

Final Phase: Promotion announcements & cooldown logging

Conclusion
Completion Speech

"We have now completed all phases of the training! I will announce the passers shortly. During this time, you may chat among yourselves (PTS Inactive)."

Cooldown / Rank Notice

"If you have been promoted from this training, your cooldown will range from 3 days to 1 week. Please check with us if you're unsure about your cooldown period."

Final Dismissal

"This concludes our training session! It has been an honor and a pleasure to be your host!"

Post-Session Administrative Checklist

  • Take group photo with all attendees visible
  • Mark passing trainees on Bloxcord portal
  • Unlock training room doors for exit
  • Submit logs in Stateview communications server
