STATEVIEW RANKING GUIDE 🧾

STATEVIEW GUIDES
RANKING FROM THE SESSION TAB

1. Checking cooldowns:

When checking cooldowns, do not rely on BloxCord's session panel, or the /cooldown command. Instead, you should enter the attendee's logbook and see when they last got promoted (only entries you can see on the logbook count, demotions don't affect cooldown, rank claims don't affect cooldown, reverted promotions don't affect cooldown)
If the time elapsed since their promotion has exceeded their cooldown period:
THEY'RE OFF COOLDOWN; promote them.
If the time elapsed since their promotion has not yet exceeded their cooldown period:
THEY'RE ON COOLDOWN; don't promote them.

2. Promoting them:

If they're off-cooldown and there's no other issues, promote them to their next rank. Sometimes BloxCord says cooldown was bypassed, but if you made sure they're off-cooldown on their logbook, you can ignore that [Cadet-Corrections Officer-Emergency Response Team-Commander-Prison Inspector-Deputy Warden-Warden-Superintedent]
If they are ranked Low Security Inmate [LSI]:
DO NOT PROMOTE THEM
If they are a different rank in-game and on BC:
Promote them to the next rank from the one you see in-game. You should promote them manuall* using the Ranking tab on BloxCord.
If session panel says they're ineligible or on cooldown:
Promote them manually; session panel can be wrong.
MANUAL RANKING
When ranking manually, you should go to the Ranking tab on BloxCord, and promote them to the next rank manually. Sometimes BC says cooldown was bypassed, but if you made sure they're off cooldown on their logbook, you can ignore that.
If BloxCord doesn't let you promote them due to you not being able to bypass cooldown:
If you have made sure they aren't on cooldown on their logbook, directly message somebody who has those BloxCord permissions to rank them for you; usually CAC+.
Don't forget to log the promotions!
